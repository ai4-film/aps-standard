# Glossary · Słownik terminologiczny

Normative for all APS translations. Where a term originates in Regulation (EU) 2024/1689
or Directive (EU) 2019/790, the **official English wording of that instrument** is used —
not a back-translation from Polish.

Normatywny dla wszystkich tłumaczeń APS. Tam, gdzie termin pochodzi z rozporządzenia
(UE) 2024/1689 lub dyrektywy (UE) 2019/790, stosuje się **oficjalne brzmienie angielskie
tego aktu**, nie tłumaczenie z polskiego.

## Copyright terms · Terminy prawnoautorskie

Polish copyright concepts do not map cleanly onto common-law terminology. Where no exact
equivalent exists, keep the concept and add a gloss on first use rather than forcing a
false equivalence.

| Polski | English | Uwaga |
|---|---|---|
| utwór | work | Art. 1(1) of the Polish Copyright Act. On first use gloss as *work within the meaning of Polish copyright law* |
| przejaw działalności twórczej o indywidualnym charakterze | manifestation of creative activity of an individual character | Statutory phrase. Translate literally, do not paraphrase as "originality" |
| twórca | author | Not "creator" in the legal sense |
| autorskie prawa majątkowe | economic rights | — |
| autorskie prawa osobiste | moral rights | Note: inalienable under Polish law — flag where relevant |
| wizerunek | likeness | Art. 81. Distinct from rights in the recording itself |
| zezwolenie na rozpowszechnianie wizerunku | likeness release | Industry term: *talent release* where the context is production |
| pola eksploatacji | fields of exploitation | Polish-law concept with no direct equivalent; keep and gloss |
| domena publiczna | public domain | **Avoid** for purely AI-generated output. Such output is not a *work* and therefore unprotected — which is not the same as being in the public domain |

## Translation rules · Zasady tłumaczenia

1. **Requirement identifiers do not change.** `APS-REG-010` is `APS-REG-010` in every
   language. Never localise, never renumber.
2. **Requirement wording is normative.** Translate meaning precisely; do not improve,
   condense or clarify. Ambiguity in the source is resolved in the source, not in
   translation.
3. **Legal citations keep their original form.** Polish statutes are cited in Polish with
   an English gloss: *ustawa o prawie autorskim i prawach pokrewnych* (Polish Copyright
   Act), art. 81.
4. **Do not add legal statements** that are absent from the authoritative version.
   Translation is not an opportunity to complete the text.
5. **Where a term has no equivalent**, keep the source term and gloss it. A false
   equivalence is worse than an untranslated term.
6. Flag any passage where faithful translation was not possible rather than resolving
   it silently.
