# Register of legal citations

A record of every statement about the law made in the standard, with its primary source
and the date it was last verified.

**Purpose:** when the legal position changes, it is clear which passages need review
without rereading the whole document. The same logic APS imposes on producers through
ToS snapshots.

**Rule:** every normative statement citing a legal provision must have an entry here with
a primary source. Commentaries, law firm briefings and secondary material are not primary
sources — at most they indicate where to look.

**Periodic review:** at least every six months and before every release.
